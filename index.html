What we fix

Services

A fully equipped service truck means most jobs get done right where you're parked. All makes: Freightliner, Peterbilt, Kenworth, Volvo, International, Mack, Western Star.

Roadside Breakdowns

Stuck on the shoulder or at a truck stop? We come out day or night, diagnose it, and get you rolling.

Diagnostics

Computer diagnostics, fault codes, check engine lights, derates and ECM troubleshooting.

Engine Repair

Cummins, Detroit, PACCAR, Volvo and more: sensors, injectors, turbos, fuel systems and leaks.

Brakes & Air Systems

Brake adjustments, chambers, slack adjusters, air leaks, air lines, valves and compressors.

Tire Service

Flat repair and tire replacement for steers, drives and trailers, right on the spot.

Electrical & Batteries

Jump starts, batteries, starters, alternators, wiring, trailer lights and pigtails.

DPF, DEF & Emissions

Forced regens, NOx and temp sensors, DEF system problems and derate issues.

Cooling & Fluids

Coolant leaks, hoses, belts, water pumps, radiators, fuel leaks and oil leaks.

Suspension & Air Bags

Air bags, leveling valves, shocks, leaf springs and fifth wheel issues.

Trailer Repair

Dry van, reefer and flatbed: lights, brakes, air lines, landing gear, doors and more.

PM & DOT Inspections

Preventive maintenance, oil changes and DOT inspections at your yard, without the downtime.

Fleet Service

From one owner-operator to large fleets: on-site repairs and scheduled maintenance.

Questions

FAQ

Are you really available 24/7?

Yes. Regular hours are 8 AM – 6 PM, but for breakdowns and emergencies you can call anytime, day or night, 7 days a week.

How far will you travel?

We cover a 200-mile radius around Vancouver, WA. That includes Portland, Seattle, Tacoma, Salem, Eugene, Yakima, Bend and everything in between.

How much does it cost?

Every job is different, so call for a quote. Tell us the problem and we'll give you an honest price before we start. No surprise bills.

Do you have a shop I can bring my truck to?

We're 100% mobile, so we come to you. That saves you a tow bill and days waiting on a shop.

What should I have ready when I call?

Your exact location (truck stop name, mile marker or a pin drop), the year/make/model of the truck or trailer, any fault codes on the dash, and a short description or photo of the problem.

Do you work with fleets?

Absolutely. We work with everyone from single owner-operators to large fleets, with on-site repairs, PM service and DOT inspections at your yard.
