Questions

FAQ

Do you have a shop I can bring my truck to?

We're 100% mobile, so we come to you. That saves you a tow bill and the time waiting on a shop. If a repair truly needs a lift or shop equipment, we'll tell you straight up and help you figure out the best next step.

How fast can you get to me?

It depends on where you are and what time it is. When you call, we'll give you a real ETA before we head out.

How much does it cost?

Every job is different. Tell us the problem and we'll give you an upfront estimate before starting, including the service call. No surprise bills.

What information should I have ready when I call?

Your exact location (a truck stop name, mile marker or pin drop), the year/make/model of the truck or trailer, any fault codes on the dash, and a short description or photo of the problem.

Do you work on trailers too?

Yes: dry vans, reefers and flatbeds. Lights, brakes, air lines, air bags, suspension and more.

Do you work with fleets?

Absolutely. We do on-site repairs and preventive maintenance at your yard. Call or send a request to set up a fleet account.
